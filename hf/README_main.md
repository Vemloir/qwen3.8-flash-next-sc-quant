# Qwen3.8-Flash-Next EXL3, self-calibrated

EXL3 quants of [Qwen/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next) converted from BF16 with
calibration data generated by the model itself (agent sessions, chats at four reasoning levels, images).

| Branch | Bits per weight | Head | KL divergence vs BF16, independent traces |
|---|---|---|---|
| [4.05bpw_sc_h8](https://huggingface.co/Vemloir/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-sc-exl3/tree/4.05bpw_sc_h8) | 4.05 | 8 | -12.4% (Nemotron conversations) and -16.9% (multilingual) against turboderp's 4.05 bpw |

Details, results and caveats are in each branch's README. Calibration data:
[agents](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Vemloir/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-calib-agents),
[chat](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Vemloir/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-calib-chat),
[multimodal](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Vemloir/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-calib-multimodal); evaluation:
[Vemloir/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-evaluation](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Vemloir/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-evaluation); code:
[Vemloir/qwen3.8-flash-next-sc-quant](https://github.com/Vemloir/qwen3.8-flash-next-sc-quant).

Requires exllamav3 >= 1.6.1.
